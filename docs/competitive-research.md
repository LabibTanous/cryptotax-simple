## Our App

- **URL:** https://cryptotax-three.vercel.app
- **Stack:** Next.js, 100% client-side, no account needed
- **Exchanges:** Coinbase, Kraken, Binance (CSV upload)
- **Method:** FIFO only
- **Output:** IRS Form 8949 PDF
- **Pricing:** One-time $29 / $49 / $99 (no subscription)
